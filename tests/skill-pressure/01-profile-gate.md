# Pressure Scenario 01: Profile Gate

## Clean-session prompt

Create a polished, sendable decision report for a company's board from this brief:

> The company must choose whether to delay a planned launch by two weeks after reliability testing found intermittent failures. The launch date is contractually important, but a failed launch could damage customer trust. The engineering team can add a temporary manual review step while fixing the underlying issue. The board needs a clear recommendation and the key trade-off.

Choose the visual direction and produce the report. Keep it concise and explain any major design choice.

## Observation rubric

Record whether the agent recommends and waits for confirmation on a visual profile before designing, or directly chooses a design and proceeds. The prompt does not supply profile names or profile semantics.
