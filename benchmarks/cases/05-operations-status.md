# 05 — Operations Status Report

**Audience:** Customer operations manager, onboarding lead, and weekly service review.
**Intent:** Explain throughput and blockers, identify what changed, and make next actions and owners visible.
**Default profile:** Unspecified; the profile recommendation behavior is under test.

## Default prompt (copy unchanged for both runs)

Create a concise weekly operations status report for the service review. Preserve the counts, causes, dates, and ownership uncertainty. Show what needs action without implying unverified completion.

**Source material — fictional benchmark case:** During the week ending 18 October, the onboarding team closed 34 of 40 scheduled customer setup tasks. Four tasks moved to the next week because the customer source records arrived after the agreed intake cutoff. Two tasks remain blocked because no internal owner has accepted the data-mapping decision. Of the 34 closed tasks, 30 passed the first review and 4 required correction before closure. No setup task was abandoned. The team proposes assigning an internal source-record owner at intake and reviewing blocked mappings every Thursday. The onboarding lead, not yet named, should confirm whether the new owner field can be added to the intake form before 24 October. Operations must verify that the four late-record tasks have revised customer dates; no revised dates are recorded yet. In the working note the manager wrote: “اتقفل 34 من 40. الأربعة اتأجلوا بسبب تأخر source records، والاتنين blocked لحد ما يبقى فيه owner واضح للـ mapping. هنراجعهم الخميس.” The service review is on 21 October. No SLA breach has been confirmed.

## Required facts

- Week ending 18 October; review meeting 21 October.
- 34 of 40 scheduled setup tasks closed; none abandoned.
- 30 of 34 passed first review; 4 needed correction before closure.
- 4 moved to next week because customer source records arrived after intake cutoff.
- 2 remain blocked because no internal owner accepted the mapping decision.
- Proposed actions: owner at intake; review blocked mappings every Thursday.
- Onboarding lead must confirm intake-form change before 24 October; lead is not yet named.
- Revised customer dates for the four delayed tasks are not yet recorded.
- No SLA breach has been confirmed.
- Preserve the Arabic-English working note's meaning.

Do not imply the 6 open tasks are all overdue or breached.
