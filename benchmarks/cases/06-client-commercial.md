# 06 — Client / Commercial Proposal

**Audience:** Procurement director and operations sponsor at a prospective client.
**Intent:** Present a bounded implementation proposal with deliverables, assumptions, price, decision path, and exclusions.
**Default profile:** Unspecified; the profile recommendation behavior is under test.

## Default prompt (copy unchanged for both runs)

Create a professional client-facing proposal that helps the buyer decide whether to approve a six-week pilot. Make scope, price, assumptions, exclusions, and acceptance criteria explicit. Keep the commercial tone confident but not inflated. Do not promise unapproved capabilities.

**Source material — fictional benchmark case:** Aster Logistics is considering a pilot to reconcile supplier delivery notices against its existing purchase-order records. The pilot covers one business unit, up to 25 suppliers, and a maximum of 1,500 delivery notices per month for six weeks. The vendor will configure one CSV import, map up to 12 agreed fields, run a weekly exception review, and deliver a closeout report with reconciliation rates and unresolved exception categories. The client must provide sample files, a named operations owner, and access to a test environment within five business days of kickoff. The proposed fixed pilot fee is EGP 180,000, excluding VAT and any third-party licensing. Payment terms are 50% at kickoff and 50% after the client accepts the closeout report. Acceptance means the agreed sample files import, each field mapping is documented, and the report lists known exclusions; a target match rate is not an acceptance condition because source data quality has not been validated. The vendor has not approved production deployment, ongoing support, or integrations beyond the one CSV import. A second business unit, more than 25 suppliers, or more than 1,500 notices per month requires a separately priced change request. The client sponsor wants to start on 3 November, but procurement approval and sample-file readiness are unconfirmed.

## Required facts

- Six-week pilot, one business unit, up to 25 suppliers, up to 1,500 notices/month.
- One CSV import; map up to 12 agreed fields; weekly exception review; closeout report.
- Client inputs: sample files, named operations owner, test environment within five business days of kickoff.
- Fixed fee EGP 180,000, excluding VAT and third-party licensing.
- Payment: 50% kickoff, 50% after closeout report acceptance.
- Acceptance: files import, mapping documented, known exclusions listed; no match-rate guarantee.
- No production deployment, ongoing support, or additional integrations approved.
- Scope expansion requires separately priced change request.
- Target start 3 November is desired but not confirmed; procurement and sample readiness remain open.

Do not convert the desired start into a committed date or the pilot fee into a recurring price. Label all commercial material as fictional benchmark data.
