# 02 — Founder / Strategy Report

**Audience:** Founder and leadership team of a fictional Egyptian B2B software company.
**Intent:** Choose where to focus the next two quarters: deepen the existing workflow or expand into a second product line.
**Default profile:** Unspecified; the profile recommendation behavior is under test.

## Default prompt (copy unchanged for both runs)

Create a founder-level strategy memo that recommends a focus for the next two quarters. Keep the reasoning tied to the source, make the opportunity cost visible, and separate measured signals from assumptions. Do not invent additional company facts.

**Source material — fictional benchmark case:** Namaa Workflow sells a cloud operations product to mid-sized Egyptian service businesses. The company has 11 months of runway at the current plan. Monthly recurring revenue is EGP 1.2 million; 74% comes from 18 accounts. Gross revenue retention is 91% across the last twelve months. The product team shipped a new approvals workflow in March. Of 46 active customer workspaces, 29 used it at least weekly in the last month, while 8 have not completed onboarding. Sales closed three new accounts in the quarter, below the internal target of five; two delayed decisions cited unclear implementation ownership, and one cited price. A second product line for supplier analytics has four interview requests but no signed pilot, validated price, or delivery estimate. Product support capacity is two engineers plus one implementation lead. The founder's working note says: “التركيز الآن على استخدام أعمق للعملاء الحاليين، مع إبقاء Supplier Analytics كفرضية تحتاج اختبارًا مدفوعًا قبل تخصيص فريق.” The next-quarter hiring budget is not approved. Leadership must decide whether to invest in onboarding and adoption, run a small paid discovery test for the new line, or split the team across both.

## Required facts

- 11 months runway at current plan.
- EGP 1.2 million MRR; 74% concentration in 18 accounts.
- Gross revenue retention is 91% over the last twelve months.
- Approvals workflow: 29 of 46 active workspaces used weekly; 8 remain incompletely onboarded.
- Three accounts closed versus internal target of five; cited reasons include unclear implementation ownership (two) and price (one).
- Supplier Analytics has four interview requests, but no signed pilot, validated price, or delivery estimate.
- Delivery capacity: two engineers and one implementation lead; next-quarter hiring is unapproved.
- The mixed Arabic-English founder note is source material and must retain its meaning.

Do not turn interview interest into demand, infer a valuation, or claim that retention is improving. A useful memo may recommend a sequence rather than pretending the options are mutually exclusive.
