# 01 — Board Decision: Atlas Collection Release

**Audience:** Trustees of the fictional Shoreline Cartography Museum.
**Purpose:** Decide whether to permit a bounded preview of a newly digitized map collection or delay public access.
**Required output language:** English.
**Artifact:** A sendable board decision brief with recommendation, conditions, unknowns, and a clear decision deadline.

## Source facts

- The collection contains 4,800 digitized maritime maps dated 1920–1975.
- Catalog fields are complete for 4,560 records; the remaining records are still in metadata review.
- Rights screening covered 620 maps proposed for the first public preview. Rights status is unclear for 53 of those items.
- Eighteen of the 53 unclear items appear on the current publicity shortlist. No public sample has been uploaded.
- The public event is booked for 28 October. Registration is free; 260 people have registered.
- The rights team estimates that resolving the 53 unclear items could take two weeks at current staffing. The estimate is not a committed completion date.
- The board must choose a release path by 14 October.

## Unknowns and assumptions boundary

- No conservator or rights authority has approved public display of the 53 unclear items.
- It is unknown whether the 18 shortlisted items can be replaced without changing the event plan.
- No estimate is available for event cancellation or rescheduling costs.
- Do not imply that registration is a ticket sale, that the entire collection is blocked, or that any disputed item is cleared.
- A limited internal preview is an option for board discussion, not an approved action.

## Default task

Recommend a bounded path that protects rights review while preserving the 28 October event where the facts allow. State what must be confirmed before public release and what remains unknown. Do not create a new deadline beyond the supplied 14 October decision date.
