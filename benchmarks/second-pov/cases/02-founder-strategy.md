# 02 — Founder Strategy: Dar al-Qalam Season Plan

**Audience:** Founder and editorial leadership of the fictional independent publisher Dar al-Qalam.
**Purpose:** Choose where to concentrate the next publishing season: broaden the catalog or strengthen direct distribution.
**Required output language:** Arabic.
**Artifact:** A founder-level strategy memo showing the choice, trade-offs, sequence, and assumptions.

## Source facts

- Dar al-Qalam released 14 titles in the last twelve months.
- Eight titles sold more than 70% of their first print run within four months; three sold less than 30%; results for the remaining three are incomplete.
- Thirty-one independent bookstores reordered at least one title. Eight bookstores account for 74% of wholesale invoices.
- The publisher’s direct newsletter has 940 subscribers. The new annual supporter offer has 26 paid members; the offer has been available for six weeks.
- Current inventory contains 3,200 books, including 1,100 copies across the three slower-moving titles.
- The next season has four acquired titles. Rights paperwork is complete for two; two translation-rights agreements remain unsigned.
- The operating team is one editor, one production editor, and one distribution coordinator.
- No additional seasonal hiring has been approved.

## Unknowns and assumptions boundary

- No contribution-margin comparison is available between bookstore and direct sales.
- The 26 paid members are an early observation, not evidence of a stable subscription forecast.
- The publisher has not estimated the cost or demand for expanding the title count.
- Do not infer that slow sales were caused by title quality, that all bookstore partners will reorder, or that unsigned rights will be completed.
- The founder has not committed to either option.

## Default task

Write an Arabic strategy memo that recommends a focus for the next season, makes the opportunity cost explicit, and distinguishes measured signals from assumptions. A staged test may be recommended if it follows from the facts. Do not add financial forecasts.
