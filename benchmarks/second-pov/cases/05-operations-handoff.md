# 05 — Operations Handoff: Tide Readers Forum

**Audience:** Friday close-out lead and Saturday opening team for the fictional Tide Readers Forum.
**Purpose:** Transfer setup status, unresolved dependencies, watchouts, owners, and the next check.
**Required output language:** English.
**Artifact:** A concise shift handoff suitable for the incoming operations team.

## Source facts

- The forum opens to visitors at 08:30 on Saturday at the fictional Annex C venue.
- Eighteen of 21 exhibitors have confirmed arrival.
- Three booth-sign proofs are still awaiting exhibitor approval.
- Hall A soundcheck finished at 17:10 Friday.
- The egress walk-through passed in Hall A. Hall B has not been signed off.
- Sixty-four staff lanyards are ready; six name corrections are still open.
- A power contractor is scheduled for a 06:45 Saturday test. No acknowledgement has been received.
- The Saturday evening lead for Hall B has not been named.
- The venue has not reported a cancellation or a safety incident.

## Unknowns and assumptions boundary

- Do not treat the contractor booking as confirmation that the test will occur.
- Do not describe Hall B as cleared.
- It is unknown whether the three sign proofs or six lanyard corrections will be complete before opening.
- No one has approved a change to the public opening time.
- Do not invent an owner for the Hall B evening shift.

## Default task

Write an English handoff that clearly separates completed work, open work, watchouts, and the next action. Keep an explicit owner gap where one is not named. Do not imply a cancellation or safety issue.
