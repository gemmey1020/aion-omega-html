# 06 — Client Proposal: Riwaq Annual Report Accessibility

**Audience:** Finance director and communications lead at the fictional Riwaq Cultural Trust.
**Purpose:** Decide whether to approve a bounded accessibility-remediation project for the annual report.
**Required output language:** Arabic.
**Artifact:** A client-facing proposal with scope, fee, inputs, acceptance conditions, exclusions, and decision path.

## Source facts

- Riwaq’s annual impact report is 18 pages in Arabic and English.
- The current source includes 12 charts or tables and three photo galleries.
- The proposed project will improve reading order, add meaningful alternative text for the 12 charts and tables, and deliver a tagged PDF plus a short HTML summary.
- The client must provide the editable source, approved figures, rights-cleared photos, and licensed brand fonts within three business days of kickoff.
- Proposed fixed fee: EGP 126,000, excluding VAT and printing.
- Proposed schedule: four weeks from receipt of the complete approved source package.
- Proposed payment: 40% at kickoff and 60% after acceptance.
- Acceptance means all 18 pages are included, the 12 charts or tables have reviewed descriptions, the PDF has a documented tag and reading-order check, and the HTML summary is delivered.
- The client would prefer a 2 November kickoff. Procurement approval and the source package are not yet confirmed.

## Unknowns and assumptions boundary

- The proposal does not include translation, fact-checking, visual rebranding, printing, or a guarantee across every assistive-technology product.
- The four-week estimate starts only after complete approved inputs arrive.
- No late-delivery penalty or tax treatment beyond VAT exclusion has been agreed.
- Do not turn the preferred kickoff date into a committed start date.
- Label the supplied fee and schedule as proposal inputs, not executed terms.
