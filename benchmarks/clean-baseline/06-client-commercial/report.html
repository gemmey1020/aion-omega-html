Supplier notice reconciliation

A bounded pilot to make delivery exceptions visible.

We propose a six-week, fixed-fee pilot to compare supplier delivery notices with existing purchase-order records, review exceptions each week, and report what reconciles and what remains unresolved.

What the pilot includes

Vendor responsibilities

  • Configure one CSV import for the agreed delivery-notice sample format.
  • Map and document up to 12 fields agreed with Aster.
  • Run a weekly exception review during the pilot.
  • Deliver a closeout report with reconciliation rates and unresolved exception categories.

Aster prerequisites

  • Provide representative sample files within five business days of kickoff.
  • Name an operations owner to coordinate field decisions and weekly reviews.
  • Provide access to a test environment within five business days of kickoff.

Proposed sequence

  1. Kickoff: confirm the business unit, sample files, operations owner, test access, and agreed fields.
  2. Configure: set up the single CSV import and document up to 12 field mappings.
  3. Review weekly: examine exceptions and record unresolved categories.
  4. Close out: submit the report of reconciliation rates and unresolved exception categories for acceptance.

Acceptance and boundaries

Acceptance criteria

Acceptance of the closeout report means:

  • The agreed sample files import.
  • Each field mapping is documented.
  • The report lists known exclusions.

A target match rate is not an acceptance condition because source data quality has not been validated.

Outside this proposal

  • Production deployment and ongoing support.
  • Any integration beyond the one CSV import.
  • Third-party licensing charges and VAT.
  • A second business unit, more than 25 suppliers, or more than 1,500 notices per month.

Scope changes: A second business unit or volumes above the stated supplier or notice limits require a separately priced change request. Production deployment, ongoing support, and additional integrations are not approved under this pilot proposal.

Start-date dependency: The sponsor’s requested 3 November start is proposed, not confirmed. Procurement approval and sample-file readiness remain unconfirmed; access and the named operations owner are also required within five business days of kickoff.
