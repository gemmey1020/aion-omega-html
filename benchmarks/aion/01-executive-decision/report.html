Board recommendation

Request a two-week delay, subject to customer approval.

This judgment gives the team another full regression cycle against an unresolved reliability issue. The account lead has not confirmed customer acceptance, so the delay is not yet secured. If approval is declined, the fallback is a constrained launch for the existing customer cohort with staffed manual review of every flagged order.
